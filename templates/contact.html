Frequently Asked Questions

Are there age requirements for services?

All services are available for people over 12 years.

Parental consent is required for any clients under the age of 16 years.

How long will results last?

Varies by service, typically...

Gel Manicure / Nails: 2-3 weeks

Acrylic Nails: 2-4 weeks

Eyebrow Waxing: 3-6 weeks

Brow and Lash tints: 3-6 weeks

Lash Lifts: 6-8 weeks

What is your return / refund policy?

We offer a 3 day return policy for UNOPENED products.

We DO NOT offer refunds for beauty services.

What is your cancellation policy?

You must provide at least 24 hours notice before an appointment if you plan to cancel.

Deposits are forfeited following failure to show up to a booked appointment.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, but keep in mind you may be waiting a while for an opening in the schedule.
